ChaitrikaBot

The friendly live chat helper for the Chaitrika is Live YouTube channel.

What it does. Chaitrika streams BGMI live in Telugu twice a day. While she is busy playing, ChaitrikaBot reads the live chat of our own streams and answers viewers' questions and comments with short, friendly replies in Telugu and English. It posts from its own moderator channel, @ChaitrikaBot, and clearly identifies itself as a bot.

It also shows our channel's subscriber count and subscriber goal on the stream overlay.

Who can use it

ChaitrikaBot is a personal tool. It runs only on the streamer's own computer and only in the live chat of the Chaitrika is Live channel. It is not sold, distributed or offered to other channels, and it shows no ads.

How it uses YouTube

  • Reads public live chat messages on our own live streams.
  • Posts replies in that live chat from the @ChaitrikaBot channel.
  • Reads public information about our own channel and stream (title, subscriber count).

Read the Privacy Policy to see exactly what data is used and how to remove access, and the Terms of Service for the rules of use.
