ChaitrikaBot

The friendly live chat helper for the Chaitrika is Live YouTube channel.

What it does. Chaitrika streams BGMI live in Telugu three times a day. While she is busy playing, ChaitrikaBot reads the live chat of our own streams and acts as a friendly host and moderator: it answers viewers' questions and comments with short replies in Telugu and English, welcomes viewers when they first chat, thanks new subscribers, channel members and Super Chats, and keeps the chat family-friendly under our chat rules. It posts from its own moderator channel, @ChaitrikaBot, at most once every 20 seconds, and clearly identifies itself as a bot.

Our stream panel, Live Desk, also shows our channel's subscriber count, subscriber goal and new-subscriber alerts on the stream overlay. It thanks our Crown Family members by name, and it is where the streamer switches the bot's moderation actions on or off.

Who can use it

ChaitrikaBot is a personal tool. It runs only on the streamer's own computer and only in the live chat of the Chaitrika is Live channel. It is not sold, distributed or offered to other channels, and it shows no ads.

How it uses YouTube

  • Reads public live chat messages on our own live streams.
  • Posts replies, welcomes and thank-yous in that live chat from the @ChaitrikaBot channel.
  • Applies our chat rules there: warns, removes rule-breaking messages, and gives short temporary timeouts (5 minutes by default) to repeat offenders. YouTube approved these actions for our project, and the streamer controls them in Live Desk.
  • Reads the membership messages YouTube posts in our chat (joins, upgrades, milestones and gifted memberships) to thank members, and to show Crown Family members' names on our stream.
  • Reads information about our own channel and streams (title, subscriber count, viewers watching) and the names of new public subscribers, with our channel's read-only sign-in.

Read the Privacy Policy to see exactly what data is used and how to remove access, and the Terms of Service for the rules of use.
