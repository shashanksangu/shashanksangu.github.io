Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026 (v3)

This policy explains what information ChaitrikaBot ("the bot", "we", "our") accesses, collects, stores, uses, shares and deletes, including API Data we receive from YouTube API Services that relates to you. ChaitrikaBot is a personal live chat helper run by Shashank Sangu for the YouTube channel Chaitrika is Live. It has two parts: the chat bot, which runs on the streamer's computer, and our stream overlay, which shows the channel's subscriber count. Both use one Google Cloud project.

YouTube API Services

ChaitrikaBot uses YouTube API Services. By chatting in our live streams where the bot is active, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Who this policy covers

  • Viewers who post in the live chat of a Chaitrika is Live stream while the bot is running. Viewers never sign in to ChaitrikaBot, and we never access a viewer's Google account.
  • The bot's own account: the @ChaitrikaBot YouTube channel, which we own. It is the only account that signs in to the bot.

API Data we access, collect, store and use

The table lists every kind of YouTube API Data the bot receives, where it comes from, why we use it, and how long we keep it.

DataSource (YouTube API)Why we use itWhere it is kept and for how long
Viewers' live chat messages: display name, channel ID, message text and time sent liveChatMessages.list on our own live stream (OAuth scope youtube.force-ssl, granted by the @ChaitrikaBot account) To decide which messages need a reply, write the reply, and keep the last ~30 messages as context so replies make sense In memory only while the bot runs during that stream. A copy is written to a daily troubleshooting log on the streamer's computer and is deleted automatically after 30 days.
Message type flags: Super Chat, moderator, channel owner liveChatMessages.list (same as above) To skip our own and moderator messages and to thank Super Chats In memory only during the stream. Not stored.
Questions for the streamer: display name, question text and time liveChatMessages.list (same as above) Viewer questions the bot can't answer are saved so the streamer can answer them in a later stream A text file on the streamer's computer. Deleted automatically after 30 days.
The bot's replies liveChatMessages.insert, posted as @ChaitrikaBot To answer viewers in our live chat Public in our YouTube live chat, as with any chat message. A copy is in the daily log and is deleted after 30 days.
Our stream details: live chat ID and stream title videos.list for our own live stream To find the chat of the stream the bot should join In memory only during the stream. Not stored.
The bot account's channel ID and name channels.list (mine=true) To check that the bot is signed in as @ChaitrikaBot before it posts In memory only. Not stored.
Our channel's subscriber count channels.list (statistics) for our own channel, using an API key To show the count and our subscriber goal on the stream overlay Refreshed every 5 minutes. OAuth access and refresh token for @ChaitrikaBot Google sign-in (OAuth 2.0), scope youtube.force-ssl To let the bot read our live chat and post replies A file on the streamer's computer. Deleted when access is revoked or the bot is retired.

We do not access or collect email addresses, phone numbers, location, payment details, private messages, viewing history, or any data from a viewer's Google account. The bot does not use cookies, trackers or analytics.

Other information (not from YouTube API Services)

The overlay shows a short thank-you with the display name of a new public subscriber. Those names come from our StreamElements account's own connection to YouTube, not from ChaitrikaBot's YouTube API access. They are kept only so the same person isn't thanked twice and the latest name can be shown, and they are deleted within 30 days. A daily count of the bot's own API quota use is also stored. It contains no information about viewers.

How we use information

  • Only to run ChaitrikaBot in our own live chat, as described in the table above.
  • We do not sell information or use it for advertising, and we do not build profiles of viewers or combine their information with other data.
  • We do not use API Data for any purpose other than the ones listed in this policy.

Who we share it with

To write a reply, the text of recent chat messages and the display names of the viewers who wrote them are sent to Google's Gemini API, which generates the reply. Google processes this under its own terms and the Google Privacy Policy. On the free tier, Google may use this content to improve its products. We do not share information with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

Storage, security and deletion

  • Everything we store is kept only on the streamer's own password-protected computer, not on any server. The StreamElements overlay data is kept in our StreamElements account.
  • Chat logs and saved questions are deleted automatically after 30 days. The bot runs this cleanup every time it starts and again at midnight.
  • We do not store or display any YouTube API Data for more than 30 days. Anything shown on the stream overlay is refreshed at least every 5 minutes.
  • When ChaitrikaBot is retired, or its access is revoked, we delete the stored token, logs and saved questions within 7 days.

Your choices and how to remove access

  • Ask us to delete your data: email sangushashanksai@gmail.com with your YouTube channel name. We will delete any stored messages and questions from you within 7 days.
  • Revoke the bot's access: the owner of a Google account that authorised ChaitrikaBot can remove its access at any time on the Google security settings page, security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Once access is revoked, the bot can no longer read or post with that account, and we delete the stored token.
  • Avoid the bot: you can block or hide the @ChaitrikaBot channel in YouTube chat. Your messages in our chat are still public on YouTube.

Children

ChaitrikaBot does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 beyond what they post publicly in our live chat, and its replies never ask for personal details.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date and version at the top of this page.
