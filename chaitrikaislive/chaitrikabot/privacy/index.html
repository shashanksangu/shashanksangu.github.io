Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026 (v5)

This policy explains what information ChaitrikaBot ("the bot", "we", "our") accesses, collects, stores, uses, shares and deletes, including API Data we receive from YouTube API Services that relates to you. ChaitrikaBot is a personal live chat helper run by Shashank Sangu for the YouTube channel Chaitrika is Live. It has two parts, both running on the streamer's own computer and using one Google Cloud project: the chat bot, which answers, welcomes and thanks viewers in our live chat and keeps it friendly, and Live Desk, our stream control panel, which feeds the stream overlay with our subscriber count and new-subscriber alerts, thanks our top-tier channel members ("Crown Family") by name on the overlay, and is where the streamer switches the bot's moderation actions on or off.

YouTube API Services

ChaitrikaBot uses YouTube API Services. By chatting in our live streams where the bot is active, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Who this policy covers

  • Viewers who post in the live chat of a Chaitrika is Live stream while the bot is running. Viewers never sign in to ChaitrikaBot, and we never access a viewer's Google account.
  • Viewers who subscribe to Chaitrika is Live with their subscriptions set to public, during one of our live streams.
  • Channel members of Chaitrika is Live whose membership messages appear in our live chat (joining, upgrading, a membership milestone, or receiving a gifted membership), and members who chat while the bot is running.
  • Our own two accounts, which are the only accounts that ever sign in: the @ChaitrikaBot YouTube channel (to read and post in our live chat) and the Chaitrika is Live channel (read-only, in Live Desk).

Other information (not from YouTube API Services)

  • Some older overlay themes show subscriber alerts through our StreamElements account's own connection to YouTube instead of Live Desk. Those names are handled by StreamElements under its own terms and are not used by ChaitrikaBot.
  • The streamer can also add a Crown Family member's name to the Live Desk list by hand, for example someone who joined while we were offline, after checking YouTube Studio. She can also hide a name from the stream or change how it is shown. A name added by hand is removed when the person is no longer a Crown Family member, or when they ask.
  • A daily count of our own API quota use is stored. It contains no information about viewers.
  • After each stream, Live Desk keeps a short summary with counts only (messages in chat, replies, new subscribers, peak viewers), no names.
  • For our weekly "most active in chat" shout-out, Live Desk counts how many messages each display name posted in the chat logs of that week when the streamer opens the list. The counts are not stored separately, and they disappear with the logs after 30 days.

How we use information

  • Only to run ChaitrikaBot and Live Desk for our own live streams, as described in the table above: replying, welcoming and thanking viewers and members, keeping our chat friendly under our chat rules, and showing our subscriber count, alerts and Crown Family names on our overlay.
  • We do not sell information or use it for advertising, and we do not build profiles of viewers or combine their information with other data.
  • We do not use API Data for any purpose other than the ones listed in this policy.

Who we share it with

To write a reply, the text of recent chat messages and the display names of the viewers who wrote them are sent to Google's Gemini API, which generates the reply. Google processes this under its own terms and the Google Privacy Policy. On the free tier, Google may use this content to improve its products. We do not share information with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

Storage, security and deletion

  • Everything we store is kept only on the streamer's own password-protected computer, not on any server. The StreamElements overlay data is kept in our StreamElements account.
  • Chat logs and saved questions are deleted automatically after 30 days. The bot runs this cleanup every time it starts and again at midnight. The list of thanked subscribers is cleaned up the same way after 28 days. Crown Family entries from chat are checked every hour and deleted once they haven't been refreshed for 30 days.
  • We do not store or display any YouTube API Data for more than 30 days. Anything shown on the stream overlay is refreshed at least every 5 minutes.
  • When ChaitrikaBot is retired, or its access is revoked, we delete the stored token, logs, saved questions and the Crown Family list within 7 days.

Your choices and how to remove access

  • Ask us to delete your data: email sangushashanksai@gmail.com with your YouTube channel name. We will delete any stored messages and questions from you within 7 days.
  • Revoke the bot's access: the owner of a Google account that authorised ChaitrikaBot can remove its access at any time on the Google security settings page, security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Once access is revoked, the bot can no longer read or post with that account, and we delete the stored token.
  • Avoid the bot: you can block or hide the @ChaitrikaBot channel in YouTube chat. Your messages in our chat are still public on YouTube.
  • Not be thanked by name: set your subscriptions to private in your YouTube settings; we only ever see public subscribers.
  • Crown Family members who don't want their name on stream: tell us in chat or by email. We hide your name at once, and delete it from the list within 7 days if you ask.
  • Disagree with a warning, removal or timeout: tell our moderators in chat or email us; the streamer and our human moderators can always reverse it.

Children

ChaitrikaBot does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 beyond what they post publicly in our live chat, and its replies never ask for personal details.
