Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy explains how ChaitrikaBot ("the bot", "we") handles information. ChaitrikaBot is a personal live chat helper run by Shashank Sangu for the YouTube channel Chaitrika is Live.

YouTube API Services

ChaitrikaBot uses YouTube API Services. By chatting in our live streams where the bot is active, you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What information we access

From viewers in our live chat (public information shown in the chat):

  • Your YouTube display name and channel ID
  • The text of your chat message and when it was sent
  • Whether the message is a Super Chat, or from a moderator or the channel owner

From the bot's own Google account (the @ChaitrikaBot channel that signs in): an access token that lets the bot read our live chat and post messages. The permission requested is the YouTube scope youtube.force-ssl. We do not access any viewer's Google account, and viewers never sign in to anything.

From our own channel: public channel and stream details such as the stream title and subscriber count.

We do not collect email addresses, phone numbers, location, payment details or private messages, and the bot does not use cookies or trackers.

How we use it

  • To decide which chat messages need a reply and to write a short reply.
  • To keep a short context of the conversation so replies make sense.
  • To show our subscriber count and goal on the stream overlay.
  • To troubleshoot the bot and stay within API limits.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, build profiles of viewers, or combine it with other data.

Who we share it with

To write a reply, the text of recent chat messages and display names is sent to Google's Gemini API, which generates the reply. Google processes this under its own terms and the Google Privacy Policy; on the free tier, Google may use this content to improve its products. We share nothing with anyone else, except if required by law.

Storage and deletion

  • Data is stored only on the streamer's own computer, not on any server.
  • Chat logs (display name, message text, time) are kept for troubleshooting and deleted within 30 days.
  • The bot's sign-in token is stored on the same computer and is deleted if the bot is retired or access is revoked.
  • Subscriber counts are refreshed regularly and not kept as history.

Your choices and how to remove access

  • Ask us to delete your data: email sangushashanksai@gmail.com with your YouTube channel name. We will delete any stored messages from you within 7 days.
  • Revoke the bot's access: the owner of a Google account that authorised ChaitrikaBot can remove its access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google security settings). Once revoked, the bot can no longer read or post with that account, and we delete the stored token.
  • Avoid the bot: you can block or hide the @ChaitrikaBot channel in YouTube chat; your messages in our chat are still public on YouTube.

Children

ChaitrikaBot does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 beyond what they post publicly in our live chat, and it does not reply with any request for personal details.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
