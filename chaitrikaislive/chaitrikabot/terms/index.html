1. YouTube terms come first

ChaitrikaBot uses YouTube API Services. By chatting in a live stream where ChaitrikaBot is active, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Your data is also covered by the Google Privacy Policy and our Privacy Policy.

2. What the bot is

ChaitrikaBot is an automated program, not Chaitrika. It is a moderator in our live chat: it answers questions, welcomes viewers, thanks new subscribers, channel members and Super Chats, sometimes asks the chat a light question when it is quiet, and helps keep the chat friendly. Its replies are generated by AI, are meant for fun and general help, and may sometimes be wrong. Nothing the bot says is a promise, offer or statement on Chaitrika's behalf, including about giveaways, money, meetings or personal matters.

3. Acceptable use

Please do not try to make the bot say abusive, sexual, hateful or harmful things, spam it, or use it to harass anyone. The bot ignores such messages, and moderators may remove messages or block users under YouTube's rules.

4. Chat rules and moderation

Our live chat is family-friendly. Please don't use bad words, post links, or spam. Spam means 6 messages within 15 seconds, or the same message 3 times within a minute.

  • First time: ChaitrikaBot reminds you of the rule in chat. Second time: a firmer reminder.
  • The bot also removes a message that breaks these rules. A viewer who breaks them a third time in the same stream gets a temporary timeout, 5 minutes by default, after which they can chat again. Warnings start fresh in every stream. The bot never bans anyone permanently, and it removes at most 40 messages and gives at most 10 timeouts per stream.
  • These actions are switched on and controlled by the streamer in Live Desk, our stream panel. She can turn them off or change the timeout length (1 to 60 minutes) at any time.
  • The rules are never applied by the bot to the streamer or our human moderators. They, and YouTube's own tools, can reverse any action. If you think the bot got it wrong, tell a moderator in chat or email us.
  • YouTube's Community Guidelines always apply as well.

5. Channel memberships

Members of our top membership level, Crown Family, have their name shown on our stream as one of the membership's perks: on a thank-you card during gameplay, on a thank-you slide on our Starting Soon, Be Right Back and Stream Ended screens, and in a one-time celebration when they join or upgrade. Members of every level also have their name scroll past in a members band on our scenes, in their level's colour. The name shown is the YouTube display name, unless you ask us to show it differently. If you'd rather not be shown, tell us in chat or by email and we'll hide it. Memberships themselves, including payments and cancellations, are handled by YouTube under its own terms. How we handle member names is described in our Privacy Policy.
