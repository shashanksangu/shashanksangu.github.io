Delete your CStream Studio account

CStream Studio, by Shashank Sangu

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open CStream Studio, go to Settings and tap your account at the top.
  2. Tap Delete account, then Delete.
  3. Google asks you to pick your account once more to confirm.

Your account and data are deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email sangushashanksai@gmail.com from the Google account (email address) you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my CStream Studio account". We'll delete your account and data within 30 days and reply to confirm. If you write from a different address, we may ask you to confirm you own the account.

What is deleted

  • Your CStream Studio sign-in account (Google account link, name, email address).
  • Your profile in our database: setup answers and account times.

We do not keep any of this after deletion. One exception: if you bought a subscription, a record of your plan and Google Play purchase is kept for 12 months after it ends, for refunds and disputes, and then deleted. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions.

What is not affected

  • Your Google account and YouTube channel: they are yours and stay as they are. Your past streams stay on YouTube until you delete them there.
  • Data on your phone (stream key, settings, logs): it was never sent to us. Uninstall the app or clear its data to remove it.

Want to delete only your setup answers and keep your account? Change them in Account › Setup answers, or email us and we'll clear them.
