Go live from your phone. Look like a pro.

CStream Studio streams your mobile game, its sound and your voice straight to YouTube. No PC needed.

Test build

CStream Studio is in testing and is not on the Play Store yet.

What it does

  • Straight to YouTube with your channel's stream key, over an encrypted connection (RTMPS).
  • Game sound and your voice, balanced, with your voice kept in the stream during in-game voice chat (optional mic helper).
  • Set up for you: five quick questions pick the right picture quality for Wi-Fi or mobile data and your mic setup.
  • A LIVE bubble over the game for volume and mute while you play.

Your data

Signing in is optional. Your stream key stays encrypted on your phone and your stream goes straight to YouTube; we never receive it. Read the Privacy Policy, the Terms of Service, or delete your account.
