Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026 (v1)

This policy explains what information the CStream Studio app ("the app", "we", "our") collects, how it is used, where it is kept, who it is shared with, and how you can see, change or delete it. CStream Studio is made by Shashank Sangu, Bengaluru, India, who is responsible for your data under this policy.

In short: Signing in is optional. If you sign in with Google, we keep your name, email address and your answers to the app's setup questions, so your setup follows you to a new phone. Your stream key, your screen, your game sound and your voice never reach us: the stream goes straight from your phone to YouTube. We don't show ads, we don't sell data, and we don't use analytics or tracking tools.

Google's sign-in service also keeps records of sign-ins (for example the time of the last sign-in) for security, as described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Kept only on your phone (whether or not you sign in)

  • Your YouTube stream key, encrypted with a key held in your phone's secure hardware (Android Keystore). It is never sent to us and never written to logs.
  • Your settings (picture quality, volumes, mic choices), your setup answers, and how many streams you have done (for the "first stream" checklist).
  • Stream logs: technical details of each stream (bitrate, connection, errors) for troubleshooting. They stay on your phone unless you choose "Share last log" and send one to someone yourself.

What we never collect

  • Your screen, game sound and voice are encoded on your phone and sent directly to YouTube using your stream key. We do not receive, record or store your stream.
  • We do not collect your contacts, photos, files, location, or what you type.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.

3. How we use information

Only to run the app's features described above: signing you in, keeping your setup in sync, and personalising your settings and dashboard. We do not use it for advertising, we do not build profiles for anyone else, and we do not sell or rent it.

4. Who we share it with

  • Google (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) stores and processes your account and setup answers for us, as our service provider, under the Firebase data processing terms.
  • YouTube receives your stream directly from your phone when you go live, under the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. What you stream is public or private according to your settings in YouTube Studio.
  • We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users or others.

We do not share your information with anyone else.

5. Security

  • All traffic to Google and YouTube is encrypted in transit (HTTPS and RTMPS).
  • Database access rules allow each signed-in person to read, change and delete only their own profile.
  • Your stream key is encrypted on your phone with a hardware-backed key.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and keep only what the app needs.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your account information is kept until you delete your account.
  • Delete in the app: Account › Delete account. Your profile and setup answers are deleted from our database and your sign-in account is removed straight away.
  • Without the app: follow the steps on Delete your CStream Studio account. We delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email.
  • Data kept only on your phone (stream key, settings, logs) is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

7. Your rights and choices

  • See your information in the app (Account), or ask us for a copy by email.
  • Correct it: change your setup answers in Account › Setup answers. Your name and email come from your Google account.
  • Delete it, as described above, and withdraw consent at any time by signing out and deleting your account. The app keeps working without an account.
  • You can also remove CStream Studio's access to your Google account in your Google account settings.

If you are in India, you have these rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. For any request or complaint, contact our grievance contact below; we will reply within 30 days.

8. Children

Signing in to CStream Studio is for people aged 18 or over. If you are under 18, you can use the app without signing in, in which case none of your information is stored on our servers. If you believe a person under 18 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Future features

We plan to offer paid features (such as removing ads, overlay packs and a Pro plan) through Google Play, and an option to link your YouTube channel with one tap. These are not in the app yet. Before any of them collects new information, we will update this policy and, where needed, ask for your consent in the app.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date and version at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
